// 02 — THE CHALLENGE

Designing a calm, comfortable VR experience for patients.

PerlabVR is meant for people who may be unfamiliar with VR and in a delicate moment. The application had to stay simple and comfortable, delivering a smooth and calming experience from the first second inside the headset.

01 / ENVIRONMENTS

Immersive Relaxation Spaces

Interactive VR environments built in Unity to help patients relax.

02 / AUDIOVISUAL

Guided Audiovisual Content

360° video and spatial audio integrated to guide the patient through the experience.

03 / USABILITY

Comfort on Standalone Headsets

Performance and usability tuned for standalone VR hardware, to keep the experience fluid and comfortable.

// 03 — EXPERIENCE FLOW

From immersion to relaxation.

The experience is built around three ideas: place the patient in a calming space, guide them with sound and image, and keep every interaction effortless.

STEP 01

Enter the environment

The patient is placed inside an immersive environment designed to be calm and welcoming.

STEP 02

Follow the guidance

Guided audiovisual content, with 360° video and spatial audio, leads the session.

STEP 03

Relax comfortably

A smooth, low-friction experience keeps the focus on the patient's wellbeing.
