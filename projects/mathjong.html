// 02 — THE GAME

Mahjong-inspired matching meets mathematical problem-solving.

MathJong: Math & Match combines Mahjong-inspired tile matching mechanics with math-based puzzles. I designed around 150 unique, handcrafted levels organized into groups of 50, featuring custom layouts representing geometric or abstract shapes, as well as recognizable objects like hearts and umbrellas.

01 / LEVEL DESIGN

Custom Layouts

Every level is distinct and handcrafted, ranging from geometric and abstract patterns to iconic silhouettes like hearts and umbrellas.

02 / DIFFICULTY CURVE

Progression & Flow

Designed to be consistently solvable while alternating between rising challenges and breathing room to prevent player frustration.

03 / GAMEPLAY LEARNING

Boosters Integration

The difficulty curve is carefully balanced to encourage the strategic use of boosters and assists during complex moments.

// 03 — PROGRESSION DESIGN

Building a satisfying journey across ~150 levels.

My work went beyond crafting individual puzzles: I oversaw the overall player progression loop. Through blocks of 50 levels, I balanced the game flow to ensure a smooth, organic difficulty curve—delivering rewarding challenges without unfair friction, while encouraging players to leverage available support tools.

STEP 01

Handcrafted Puzzles

Manual conception of every single layout featuring diverse themes and shapes.

STEP 02

Balanced Curve

Calibrated alternation of increasing and decreasing difficulty for a satisfying gameplay flow.

STEP 03

Engagement & Boosters

Structured progression designed to encourage booster utilization and maintain long-term motivation.
