// 02 — THE GAME

Mahjong-inspired matching meets mathematical problem-solving.

MathJong: Math & Match combines strategic tile-matching mechanics with equations and number-based puzzles. Players progress through handcrafted levels, solve challenges with limited moves, and learn to use special tiles and boosters as the game gradually introduces greater complexity.

01 / LEVEL DESIGN

Handcrafted puzzles

Designed around 150 levels, balancing puzzle layouts and objectives to create varied challenges across the game.

02 / DIFFICULTY CURVE

Progressive challenge

Structured the level sequence so difficulty builds over time instead of overwhelming players too early.

03 / GAMEPLAY LEARNING

Mechanics and mastery

Considered the pace at which mechanics, special tiles and boosters appear, supporting player learning and continued engagement.

// 03 — PROGRESSION DESIGN

Building a satisfying journey across ~150 levels.

My work went beyond creating individual puzzles: I also structured how players move from one level to the next. The aim was to provide a gradual learning curve, keep challenges varied, and introduce complexity at a pace that supports both understanding and mastery.

STEP 01

Design the challenge

Create levels around the game's tile-matching and mathematical puzzle mechanics.

STEP 02

Shape the difficulty

Arrange challenges into a progression that develops player confidence and skill.

STEP 03

Maintain variety

Use changing puzzle demands and the gradual introduction of mechanics to sustain engagement.
