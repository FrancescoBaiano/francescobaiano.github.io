// 02 — THE CHALLENGE

Bringing stage storytelling into a virtual environment.

Headset Party lets users pick a video and watch it in 360° inside the headset. In the Pillole di Teatro version the content is theatre: the goal was to make stage performances easy to reach and engaging for a wider audience.

01 / SELECTION

Choose a Video in VR

Users select the video to watch directly from inside the application.

02 / PLAYBACK

Immersive 360° Playback

The selected video is played in 360°, so the viewer is placed in the middle of the scene.

03 / ACCESSIBILITY

Pillole di Teatro, created with professional actors, enhances accessibility and audience engagement.

What this project demonstrates.

Headset Party shows my work on immersive media in Unity: integrating 360° video, building a simple selection flow with VR controllers, and keeping playback usable on standalone headsets. Pillole di Teatro applies it to culture, bringing theatre to people through VR.
