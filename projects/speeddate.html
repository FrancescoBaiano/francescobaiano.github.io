// 02 — CONCEPT & GOALS

A guru who gives all the wrong advice.

Antoine is a dating guru with a new client: Eugene, who he must guide through a speed date. Through an earpiece, the player advises Eugene on how to approach each girl and how to answer her questions. But Antoine is only a fake expert, so every piece of advice is, in one way or another, a terrible idea that leads the girl to reject Eugene — even if the two protagonists rarely notice it. Each date has a limited duration, marked by the sound of a bell.

The mood is light and funny, with the goal of ridiculing the machista view of women and the figure of the dating guru. The emotion we aim to evoke in the player is cringe (fremdschämen).

01 / TOLERANCE BAR

Patience Under Pressure

Each girl has a tolerance bar that drops over time and with every wrong answer, while the right ones raise it slightly.

02 / THE NOTEBOOK

Four Answers, One Gem

For every line the girl says, the player picks Eugene's reply from 4 options tied to the topic. Three lower her tolerance; one is cringe enough to be funny and slightly raises it.

03 / WIN & LOSE

Two Ways to Be Rejected

Win: keep her at the table for 5 exchanges until the bell rings and she finds a fake excuse to reject you. Lose: tolerance hits 0 and she storms off before time runs out.

// 03 — GAME LOOP

From the first hello to the bell.

Every date follows the same short loop, built around timed back-and-forth dialogue and the choices the player whispers into Eugene's ear.

STEP 01

Arrival & Introductions

The girl sits at the table and the two introduce themselves, with Antoine whispering the opening line.

STEP 02

Back & Forth

She speaks, the player consults the notebook and picks one of four replies, then watches her tolerance react.

STEP 03

Bell or Walkout

After 5 exchanges the bell rings and she makes a polite excuse — or, if tolerance reaches zero, she leaves angrily.
