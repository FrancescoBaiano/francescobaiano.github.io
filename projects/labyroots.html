LabyRoots.

// 02 — THE GAME

A maze where every defeat leaves a mark on the next attempt.

The player enters a labyrinth created by an alien meteorite and has a limited time to destroy the roots that protect the heart of the intruder. If time runs out, the roots absorb the player and a fruit tree grows in their place: a landmark that slows the infection nearby and offers fruits with bonuses or maluses to discover. The design problems I worked on were the following.

01 / MAZE DESIGN

From the first maze to procedural ones

I defined the criteria for the first maze and then the criteria that procedurally generated mazes had to follow.

02 / READABILITY

Objectives visible inside a maze

I specified how the wall models should be built so that the objectives stay visible even while the player is lost in the labyrinth.

03 / TIME PRESSURE

When the player must restart

I set the time the player has before restarting, and how often fruits appear to delay that moment.

// 03 — CORE LOOP

Explore, destroy, survive, or leave a tree.

The GDD defines a loop built around one resource, time, and a handful of verbs: walk, pick up, drop, eat fruits and break things with the axe.

STEP 01

Explore against the clock

The infection advances while the player looks for the roots scattered in the maze. Eating the guide fruit lights up the underground roots and shows the way to them.

STEP 02

Destroy with the axe

The axe breaks the roots, cuts through roots that block shortcuts, and can also remove the landmarks the player leaves behind. This gives a reason to carry it everywhere.

STEP 03

Win, or become a tree

Destroying all the roots and then the heart wins the game. If time runs out, the player is absorbed and a tree grows there: a landmark that slows the infection and produces two fruits.

// FRUIT SYSTEM, AS DESIGNED

Fruits give bonuses or maluses that the player has to discover. In the design, the meaning of each fruit can change for every new player, so knowledge is learned in play and not memorized from a guide.

BLUE

Rewinds the infection

Moves the infection back by one stage. The rare version rewinds it completely.

YELLOW

Advances the infection

A trap: it moves the infection forward one stage. The rare version kills instantly.

RED

Reveals the roots

Makes the guide roots visible for a limited time. The rare version lasts longer.

GREEN

Speeds the player up

Teleports the player further for a limited time. The rare version lasts longer.

// 04 — MY CONTRIBUTION

What I worked on.

01

Game Design Document

Wrote the whole GDD: concept, mechanics, win and lose conditions, game elements and the task list for the team.

02

Maze criteria

Defined the criteria for the first maze and for the procedural ones, with a grid where each cell's number says what should spawn there.

03

Wall models and visibility

Specified how walls should be modeled so the objectives remain visible while the player is inside the maze.

04

Time and fruit pacing

Set the time available to the player and how often fruits appear to delay the moment they have to restart.

05

Data-driven tuning

Planned a JSON with all the design values (speed, maze setup and more), so that balance can be changed without touching code.
