// 02 — THE NARRATIVE & PREMISE

A bleak daily routine driven by guilt and experimental tests.

The scientist is the father of a boy suffering from an incurable disease treated only with experimental drugs. Consumed by guilt and psychological strain, the protagonist lives each day like an hallucination, trapped in a facility performing tests on an evolving, amorphous entity inside a capsule, as if feeding the disease itself through his research.

01 / THE ROUTINE

The Daily Work Cycle

Wake up, check emails from family and employers on the retro PC, receive instructions from the NPC, and execute complex scientific lab tasks.

02 / SURVIVAL & ECONOMY

Managing Expenses

Earn money based on test accuracy and resource efficiency, spending credits on food, electricity, facility upkeep, and narrative choices.

03 / PSYCHOLOGICAL TOLL

Branching Endings

Decisions and messages received from unknown contacts slowly shape Carlo's sanity, leading to multiple distinct narrative conclusions.

// 03 — CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

From the dormitory to the laboratory.

The gameplay combines first-person exploration, precise object interaction, and laboratory puzzle mechanics (such as Finding Resonance, Draining procedures, and Laser Scanning).

STEP 01

Morning Routine

Wake up in the room, check the retro PC for work instructions or unsettling emails, and prepare for the shift.

STEP 02

Lab Experimentation

Interact with the NPC, consult the active task list on the notepad, and handle equipment like shakers, reagents, and injectors.

STEP 03

Delivery & Rest

Deliver results at the delivery desk, receive daily earnings and expenses reports, and return to sleep before the cycle repeats.

// 04 — MY CONTRIBUTION

What I worked on.

01

Asset Creation

Modeled key props and laboratory equipment from scratch, including the containment capsule, retro computer, reagent machines, and storage vials.

02

Texturing

Handled the texturing pipeline for all created assets, giving the facility an atmospheric, gritty industrial look.

03

Level Design

Designed the functional layout of the facility, connecting the player's bedroom, bathroom, and laboratory space for optimal flow.

04

Game Design & Progression

Structured the daily game loop, economy management, and the progression curve balancing player survival and narrative tension.

05

Tutorial Design

Designed the opening onboarding sequence—from waking up in the dark corridor to acquiring the purple ID card and receiving first lab instructions.
