// 02 — THE CHALLENGE

A safe place to practice nursing skills in VR.

Nursing students need to practice on real situations before they face them. NurseLab recreates a realistic hospital room in VR, giving them a safe and controlled learning experience where mistakes cost nothing.

01 / ENVIRONMENT

Realistic Hospital Room

A believable hospital setting that students can explore in an immersive way.

02 / INTERACTION

Patients & Free Movement

Users move freely in the room and interact with patients through VR controllers.

03 / PRACTICE

Medical Equipment Training

Hands-on practice with medical equipment, in a safe and controlled setting.
